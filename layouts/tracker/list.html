What this is not

These are issued permits, not applications. A permit is issued only after an application has worked through the queue, so the most recent months are undercounted and always will be. Counts of multiplex applications run higher and earlier. The two are different measures and should not be read against each other.

The filter is text matching. A permit counts here when the City's own project description mentions a multiplex. That is accurate to within a few percent, not exact: it catches a small number of records that mention the word without being one, and misses any multiplex described without it.

A permit is not a finished home. Nothing here says how many of these were built, how many homes each contains, who lives in them, or what they cost.
