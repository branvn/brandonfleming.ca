Bill 44 requires BC municipalities to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on
most single-family lots. Implementation is happening one council agenda at a time,
across 21 municipalities in Metro Vancouver, and very little of it is reported.

This page tracks it. A script surfaces candidate items from municipal agendas and
regional news; every entry published here has been read and annotated by me. The
note is the point. An unreviewed feed would just be noise.
