This is the literature my thesis sits in, laid out in time: the reforms, and the
studies that measured what they did. Most of it is about places that upzoned
before Vancouver did, which is the point. Auckland, Minneapolis and Chicago are
where the evidence on R1-1-style reform comes from.

Studies link to a free version wherever one exists. The list at the bottom is
not chosen by me: it is the newest peer-reviewed, open-access research on
upzoning, refreshed every week.
