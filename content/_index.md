I'm a Master of Urban Studies student at Simon Fraser University, working toward
an Advanced Certificate in GIS at BCIT. My research asks a simple question about
Vancouver's 2023 R1-1 rezoning, which let most single-family lots build
multiplexes: how many homes did it actually add? The work pairs a
difference-in-differences analysis of City permit data with interviews with the
builders who used it.

Early answer: more than nothing and less than the argument for it assumes. The
rate of redevelopment didn't change. What changed is how many homes go up each
time a lot turns over.

Before that I spent a term at the Agricultural Land Commission auditing land-use
applications and mapping where farmland is quietly disappearing.

Currently looking for a graduate co-op term in municipal community or
development planning.
